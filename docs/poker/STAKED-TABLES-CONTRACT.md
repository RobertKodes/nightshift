Build and deploy PokerTableEscrow on Ethereum mainnet, for NIGHTSHIFT's staked NIGHT POKER Sit & Go tables of 2 to 6 players: every player pays the same buy-in into the table, the server plays the tournament and signs the final standings, and the contract pays the places. Player money can only go to the places of a table the player joined, or back to the player.

Assets: native ETH (address(0)), IMD 0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7, SHIFT 0xe5e58e53d0bb204aa62c995a6c8cc8a48e60c3ac (ERC-20, 18 decimals, no transfer fee). Every function works for all three; ETH by call with a checked result.

Tables: createTable(tableId, asset, buyIn, seats, payoutBps[], joinBy, deadline, operatorSig) with EIP-712 Table(bytes32 tableId,address asset,uint256 buyIn,uint8 seats,uint16[] payoutBps,uint64 joinBy,uint256 deadline) from the operator, so only tables the server deals exist; anyone may submit it. tableId new; seats 2 to 6; payoutBps has 1 to seats entries, descending, summing to 10000 (e.g. [10000] winner takes all, [6500,3500] for 6); joinBy at most 1 h ahead. join(tableId) pays exactly buyIn (transferFrom or msg.value), once per wallet, until the table is full; the last seat sets filledAt. leave(tableId) refunds a player's buy-in before the table is full. Events TableCreated, Joined, Left, Filled.

Settlement: settle(tableId, standings[], deadline, sig) with EIP-712 Standings(bytes32 tableId,address[] standings,uint256 deadline) from the result signer: standings lists every seated player once, first place first. Pot = buyIn × seats; 5% to the FeeSplitter 0x1119d5df2f046bc2d3c0f6279ef2e9ce49422d44 (it takes ETH and tokens); the rest by payoutBps to standings[0], standings[1]…, rounding dust to first place. voidTable(tableId, deadline, sig) with Void(bytes32 tableId,uint256 deadline) from the result signer refunds every buy-in, no fee. Each table settles or refunds once; a signature is good only before its deadline and before filledAt + 24 h. Events Settled(tableId, standings, payouts, fee), Voided.

Refunds without the server: a table not full by joinBy: refundUnfilled(tableId), by anyone, returns every buy-in. A full table with no settlement 24 h after filledAt: refundSilent(tableId), by anyone, returns every buy-in, no fee. Both work while paused.

Caps against a compromised signer: per asset a max buy-in (default 1000 IMD, 50000000 SHIFT, 0.5 ETH) and a min (0.5 IMD, 10000 SHIFT, 0.001 ETH); per asset a cap on the value held in open tables (default 20000 IMD, 1000000000 SHIFT, 10 ETH); createTable and join revert above them. The owner lowers caps at once; raising takes 24 h. Events CapsSet.

Roles: operator and result signer are set by the contract owner 0x6c4c78f2c3bdf4c4318fe305fb1c8bc1694d19b5 (initially the game signer the server shows on /setup); each change takes effect after 48 h, and a pending change is visible. The owner may pause createTable and join; nothing else. Nobody can move a buy-in except by the rules above, or change the fee, its recipient or the assets. No upgradeability, delegatecall, selfdestruct or sweeping. Reentrancy guard, checks-effects-interactions, SafeERC20, Solidity 0.8.x, NatSpec, events everywhere. EIP-712 domain "NightshiftPokerTables", version "1", chain 1.

Views: tables(tableId) (asset, buyIn, seats, payoutBps, joinBy, filledAt, state), players(tableId), hashTable, hashStandings, hashVoid, caps(asset), heldOpen(asset), pending role changes.

Foundry tests for ETH, IMD and SHIFT: createTable needs the operator's signature and fresh terms (bad seats, bad payout sums, joinBy too far, reused id); join exact amount, twice by one wallet, past joinBy, on a full table; leave before full; settle 2, 3 and 6 players with [10000] and [6500,3500], exact payouts, 5% fee, dust to first; standings with a stranger, a duplicate or a missing player revert; wrong signer, expired deadline, past filledAt + 24 h, double settle; void refunds all, no fee; refundUnfilled and refundSilent by anyone, also while paused, never twice; caps and the open-value cap; pause; 48 h role delays and 24 h cap raises; the owner cannot take funds; fuzz over seats and payouts; invariant: buy-ins held equal the sum over open tables, and nothing leaves except payouts, the fee and refunds.

What the server must enforce before it signs (off chain, for staked multi-player tables): one seat per wallet cluster at a table, a cluster being wallets linked by a funding transfer between them, a shared funding source in the last 30 days, the same signed-in session, device or IP; no two seats whose buy-ins were funded from one wallet (checked on the join transactions before the deal; such a table is voided, never dealt); random seat order; no live hole cards to anyone but their owner, spectators only after showdowns; the provably fair deck of NIGHT POKER (commit before the deal, every player's seed, seeds revealed after the tournament). Chip-dumping and soft-play detection, flagged to the admin on /setup with the hand histories, never acted on automatically: chips flowing again and again from one player to the same other one; all-ins called or made with very weak hands into one player; folds of strong hands to one player only; two players who never raise each other; machine-even action timings. The admin may hold a flagged table's settlement and have the server sign void instead, within the 24 h window.
